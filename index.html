Frequently asked questions

Does Pär send my data to the cloud?

No. By default, Pär Desktop runs entirely on your Mac. Your conversations, memory, and files never leave your device unless you explicitly choose a different deployment mode.

What models can I use?

Pär runs quantized GGUF models natively through llama.cpp and can also use Ollama models. You choose which models to load — no external account needed.

Is there a Windows or Linux version?

Pär Desktop currently targets macOS. Pär Web — which runs in any modern browser against a local backend — is the fastest path to Windows and Linux support and is coming soon.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Every tier supports exporting your conversations, memory, profile, and personas to a portable JSON bundle. Pro and above also include structured export formats like Markdown, CSV, PDF, and .ics, plus confirmed deletion certificates on Enterprise.

How does licensing work?

A single license key is tied to your email and works across Pär Desktop, Web, and Cloud. Activation is local and persists across app restarts via your OS keychain.

System requirements

  • macOS 11 (Big Sur) or later
  • Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer)
  • 8 GB RAM minimum (16 GB recommended)
  • 2 GB free disk space for the app; additional space for models
  • Optional: Ollama installed for Ollama model support

Beta builds are currently unsigned. On first launch, right-click the app and select Open, then confirm in System Settings → Privacy & Security if prompted.
